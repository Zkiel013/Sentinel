How to read an entry

Direction, not confidence

A detector only ever reports long, short or nothing. It never says how likely the move is — that judgement happens later, in the score.

Strength is fixed per setup

Most detectors emit a constant strength (0.5–0.8). Only volume_spike, liquidity_grab and rsi_sr_confluence scale it with how extreme the reading is.

Weight is separate

Weight decides how much the setup moves the confluence score. liquidity_grab carries 15, the most; session_open_volatility carries 6, the least.

Everything is ATR-relative

Distances are expressed in ATR, never in dollars, so the same rule behaves the same on BTC at 64,000 and ETH at 1,900 and in quiet or wild conditions.

How the nine score components read

aggregate direction

Detectors are only one input. The headline score comes from nine weighted components, each producing a signed value from −1 to +1. Positive is always bullish. The weighted mean is the net direction; buy score is 50 + 50 × net, and sell score is 101 − buy.

Volatility is deliberately absent from that list. It has no direction — it multiplies the final score down (to 0.72× in extreme conditions, 0.80× during a squeeze, 0.85× when one ATR barely clears trading costs) rather than voting either way.

What this document is not. A detector firing means a pattern printed. It is not a prediction and not advice. Backtesting in this repo found these setups lose money after realistic costs, and the measured trade gate cuts the loss roughly in half without turning it positive. Read this to understand what the tool is telling you, not as a reason to take the trade.
